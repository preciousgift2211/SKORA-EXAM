WE'RE HERE TO HELP

Help centre

What is the difference between Practice, Mock and Test?

Practice gives immediate feedback and explanations without a final score. Mock gives a final score without corrections. Test gives a score and corrections after submission.

Can I use AI during Mock or Test?

No. AI and dictionary tools are unavailable during active timed exams.

How are subject colours calculated?

Proposed bands: Green 70–100%, Yellow 50–69%, Red 0–49%. Production must use enough eligible subject-level results before assigning a label.
